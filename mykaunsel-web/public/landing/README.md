# Pictures and videos for the main page

Put a file in this folder, named after its **slot**, and it appears in its place by itself. No code to change.
Allowed types: `mp4`, `webm` (videos), `webp`, `jpg`, `png`, `svg` (pictures). If both exist, a video wins.
Until a file is here, the page shows a dashed box that says what goes there.

| File name (without the type) | Where | Shape | Suggested size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero` | Top of the page, next to the headline | 1:1 (a wider picture is cropped from the sides) | 1400 × 1400 |
| `solution-verified` | "Solution": verified counselors card | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 |
| `solution-booking` | "Solution": simple booking card | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 |
| `solution-private` | "Solution": private and safe card | 4:3 | 1200 × 900 |
| `how-it-works` | "How it works" (a short demo video suits it) | 16:10 | 1600 × 1000 |
| `audience-org` | "For organizations" card | 16:10 | 1600 × 1000 |
| `audience-counselor` | "For counselors" card | 16:10 | 1600 × 1000 |
| `app-screen` | Inside the phone, "Get the app" (dark section) | 9:19 | 1080 × 2280 |
| `app-qr` | QR code, "Get the app" (when the app is published) | 1:1 | 400 × 400 |
| `menu-platform` | Small card in the Platform menu | 16:9 | 640 × 360 |
| `menu-solutions` | Small card in the Solutions menu | 16:9 | 640 × 360 |
| `menu-pricing` | Small card in the Pricing menu | 16:9 | 640 × 360 |

Tips: keep videos short (under 15 seconds), muted, and under 8 MB. They play automatically, loop and stay silent.
Use `webp` for pictures where you can: it is smaller than `jpg` and `png`.
